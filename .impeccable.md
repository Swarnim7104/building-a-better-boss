## Design Context

### Users
Students, professors, and professionals reviewing an academic/corporate case study ("Building a Better Boss"). The context is an educational presentation, where clarity, authority, and engaging narrative flow are paramount.

### Brand Personality
Analytical, Premium, Authoritative, yet Accessible. It blends Google's data-driven engineering culture with a high-end editorial case-study format. 

### Aesthetic Direction
An "Impeccable" take on the Google Design Language. Moving away from generic Material Design cards to an editorial, magazine-like digital experience. Focus on fluid typography, asymmetrical grid-breaking layouts, sophisticated motion (exponential deceleration), and tinted neutral palettes (no pure black/white). Bold, intentional, and highly polished.

### Design Principles
1. **Editorial Quality**: Treat each slide as a magazine spread rather than a generic card.
2. **Distinctive Typography**: Avoid standard Roboto/Inter. Use premium geometric/humanist alternatives with fluid scaling.
3. **Tinted Neutrals & Purposeful Color**: Use OKLCH for perceptually uniform colors. Accents should be rich, not neon.
4. **Natural Motion**: All transitions must use exponential easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) for a cinematic, high-quality feel.
